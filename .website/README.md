AFTERGLOW is a survivors-like where your rainbow trail is the weapon. Shadows that touch your afterglow burn away, so standing still means zero firepower — keep galloping to keep killing. Every field you cross blooms back to color, so running is both how you fight and how you heal the world.

Collect shards to level up and pick 1 of 3 upgrades. Elites drop luck items — magnet, bomb, rainbow rush, star shower, heart.

Move: WASD / arrows / drag anywhere / gamepad. M = mute, R = restart.

No asset files in the zip: procedural canvas art on a fake-3D sky island, ZzFX effects, and a WebAudio sequencer playing a 6/8 Celtic lilt. One build serves desktop and mobile. No engine, no framework — only ZzFXMicro (MIT).

Source: https://github.com/Taehun/afterglow-js13k
